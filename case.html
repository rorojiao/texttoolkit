Case Converter FAQ

What case formats can I convert text to?

You can convert text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case with a single click.

Is the case converter free?

Yes, completely free. No account or software installation required — paste your text and click a button.

Can I use this converter for programming variable names?

Absolutely. camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case conversions are especially useful for developers naming variables, functions, or CSS classes.
