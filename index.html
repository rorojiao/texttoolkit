Frequently Asked Questions about TextToolKit

What text tools does TextToolKit offer?

TextToolKit provides a word counter, case converter, text cleaner, Lorem Ipsum generator, and an online Markdown editor — all free, no sign-up required.

Is TextToolKit free to use?

Yes, all tools on TextToolKit are completely free. No registration or download is required; everything runs in your browser.

Does TextToolKit store my text data?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. TextToolKit never sends your text to any server, ensuring complete privacy.
