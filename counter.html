Word Counter FAQ

How does the online word counter work?

Paste or type text into the input box and the counter instantly calculates words, characters, sentences, lines, and Chinese characters in real time.

Can this tool count characters without spaces?

Yes. The word counter displays total characters and characters excluding spaces simultaneously.

Does the word counter support Chinese text?

Yes. It detects and counts Chinese characters separately from English words, making it useful for bilingual content.
